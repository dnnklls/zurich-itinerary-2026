07:30 — collect the car.Book ahead

Paperwork takes 20–30 minutes. Three things or you don't drive away:

1. The named driver's own credit card. They hold CHF 1,000–3,000. Debit is refused.
2. Driving licence. Bring an IDP if anyone has one.
3. Check the vignette is on the car. Rentals include it. Without one you're fined.

If things change

Plan B, C, and rain.

Drop down a level, guilt-free.

If it's pouring

Check before you commit 2h10 of driving.

  • Ring Pfingstegg. The run closes when wet.
  • Low cloud means First shows you nothing.
  • If the Oberland is socked in, cancel the car.
  • Train to Rapperswil instead, or stay in Zürich.

If you're tired (Plan B)

4h20 of driving is a lot two days off a red-eye.

  • Don't drive tired. Someone else drives, or nobody goes.
  • Train to Rapperswil — 35 min, lake town, castle.
  • Come back by boat and sit down for the whole hour.
  • Early dinner, home by nine.

If you're wrecked (Plan C)

Protect the sleep and the flight home.

  • Don't leave Glattpark.
  • Gym and health club are free to crew.
  • Crew lounge runs 24 hours.
  • Walk the Glattpark lake and call it a day.

Before you land

Logistics.

Phone and apps

Nothing is blocked. Switzerland is not in the EU, so EU roaming bundles don't cover it — check your plan. Apps: ZVV for trams, SBB Mobile for trains.

Paying for things

Cards work everywhere, including trams. It's expensive: lunch CHF 25–30, beer CHF 8. Duty free back in is CHF 300 of goods.

September weather

21°C by day, 13°C after dark. First is at 2,168 m and will be much colder — take a proper jacket. Rain about one day in three.

Getting around

Tram 10 does everything: city in 30 min, airport in 9. 24-hour ticket CHF 14.40. Buy before boarding — inspectors fine on the spot. Trams stop around midnight.

Driving in Switzerland

Motorways need a vignette, CHF 40 — rentals include it, confirm at the desk. Limits: 120 / 80 / 50. Cameras everywhere. Headlights on always. Driver drinks nothing.

Watch out for

Pickpockets at the main station and airport. Avoid Platzspitz park, behind the Landesmuseum, and Langstrasse after dark. The 10-hour alcohol rule is enforced with random tests.

Crew perks at the Kameha

Breakfast CHF 19, lunch CHF 24. 20% off all food and drink. 30% off laundry. Free gym. 24-hour crew lounge. Room service 06:00–23:00. Non-smoking — CHF 400 penalty.
